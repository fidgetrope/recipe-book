Stafford's Seeded Loaf

A small-tin seeded loaf. Metric, made with strong flours. Makes one loaf.

  • Makes1 small loaf
  • Hands-on15 mins kneading
  • Oven170C fan
  • Tinabout 20 x 10 x 6cm

Ingredients

Method

  1. Mix both flours, the yeast, caraway seeds, salt and sugar in a bowl. Rub in the butter, then add the warm water and bring it together into a dough.

  2. 10 minsKnead well. Cover and leave to rest for 20 minutes.

  3. 5 minsKnead again. Cover and rest for another 20 minutes.

  4. Knock the dough back, shape it and place it in a greased tin, about 20 x 10 x 6cm.

  5. 45 to 60 minsLeave somewhere warm to rise until it has puffed up above the rim of the tin.

  6. Bake in a fan oven at 170C (see below). Turn out and cool on a wire rack.

Bake time 30 minutes at 170C fan

For a better rise and a softer crust, put a tray of boiling water on the bottom of the oven while the loaf bakes. It is optional, but it adds humidity.

Lovely sliced warm, with butter.
