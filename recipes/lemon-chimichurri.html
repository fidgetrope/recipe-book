Lemon Chimichurri

A fresh, dairy-free sauce for steak and chips, with no red wine vinegar. Enough for two steaks.

  • Serves2 steaks
  • Prep10 mins
  • Rest10 mins
  • Cookingnone

Ingredients

Method

  1. Stir the parsley and garlic into the olive oil.

  2. Add the lemon juice, balsamic, oregano, salt, pepper and chilli flakes if using.

  3. Taste. Add more lemon if it needs more zing, or a little more balsamic if it needs mellowing.

  4. 10 minsLeave for about 10 minutes so the flavours settle, then spoon over the steaks.

A note on the vinegar Balsamic is sweeter and darker

Chimichurri normally uses red wine vinegar. Balsamic makes a slightly sweeter, darker sauce, so start with 1 tsp and let the lemon do most of the work. White wine vinegar also works in place of the lemon.

Lovely with steak and chips.
